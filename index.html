Hi! I'm Joy Sarker. I'm interested in AI coding agents: why they sometimes give wrong answers, and how to catch these mistakes.

I'm a Software Engineer II at Therap (BD) Ltd., the Bangladesh affiliate of Therap Services, a US company that makes documentation, billing and case-management software for human services providers. I build features in a large production Java codebase with Spring MVC, JSP and JavaScript, and every feature needs JUnit and Playwright tests. I'm currently fixing security issues, where each fix also needs a test that shows it works.

Before I started working, I did competitive programming, with three ICPC Asia Dhaka Regional Honorable Mentions and more than 2,000 problems solved. I have also written more than 10 contest problems, each with test data that separates correct solutions from wrong ones.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from State University of Bangladesh (SUB) with a CGPA of 3.96/4.00, and received the Dean's Award (Silver Medal) at the 6th SUB Convocation in 2022.

research interests

I use AI coding agents every day, in my own projects and at work, where we now use them to build features. The work still takes time, and when an agent has a lot of context it sometimes hallucinates, gives wrong information, or goes around in circles without making progress. I want to study why coding agents fail in these ways and how to catch their mistakes.
